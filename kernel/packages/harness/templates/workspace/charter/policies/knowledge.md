# Policy — knowledge

Load when recording or retrieving what past work taught.

**Knowledge answers: what did work teach us that saves effort next time.** Lifespan: until
superseded. `~/ocean/05-knowledge/<kind>/`.

Knowledge exists to **cut tokens**: retrieving one compact entry beats re-reading old files
and re-deriving what was already established.

## Kinds

`task-results/` · `technical-solutions/` · `decisions/` · `architecture/` · `research/` ·
`discoveries/` · `failures/`

## Before investigating

Search `knowledge/` **before** investigating from scratch. **Check `decisions/` before
proposing something that contradicts a past decision** — that is the section's whole
purpose. `knowledge/README.md` is the index.

## Writing

Small and factual. An entry that has to be skimmed has failed. No transcripts, no
narration. Mark verification honestly: `verified` (we ran it) · `inferred` · `unverified` ·
`outdated`. A guess recorded as verified is worse than no entry.

## Deduplicate

Do not store the same content in a session record, a task, knowledge, memory and the
daily log. **One authoritative artifact, plus references.** A decision is stored once; the
task references it, the session references it, and the daily log records only that the
decision was made.
